\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Federated learning (FL) trains a shared model without centralising
client data~\cite{mcmahan2017fedavg}, often the only legally viable
option in consumer IoT. But GDPR Articles~7 and~17 demand
purpose-granular, revocable consent whose withdrawal propagates into the
model. Federated unlearning (FUL) erases a client's data and influence,
yet a bare withdrawal is a one-shot trigger, not a scoped state binding
round selection, so training on revoked data may continue unrecorded;
and approximate unlearning, the only kind tractable at FL scale, offers
no guarantee, fails \emph{silently}, and is self-reported by the
controller that owes it. \sysname couples \emph{dynamic consent} with
\emph{verifiable machine unlearning} in one chain-enforced protocol,
closing both gaps. Prior work covers single corners:
MedRec~\cite{azaria2016medrec} cannot gate training;
SISA~\cite{bourtoule2021sisa}/FedEraser~\cite{liu2021federaser} leave
trigger and verification off-chain;
blockchain-FL~\cite{kim2020blockchained,li2020bflc} adds neither consent
nor forgetting verification.

\sysname makes four contributions: (1)~a Consent Lifecycle State Machine
(\clsm) of six states and nine transitions with four stated safety and
liveness properties, as a soulbound ERC-721; (2)~a Verifiable Unlearning
Protocol (\vup), method-agnostic over the unlearning algorithm,
accepting an attestation iff three \emph{independent} MIAs
(shadow~\cite{shokri2017mia}, LiRA~\cite{carlini2022lira},
label-only~\cite{choquettechoo2021labelonly}) fall below $\tauMIA$ under
$t$-of-$n$ ECDSA signatures; (3)~a three-contract Solidity architecture
(ConsentRegistry, UnlearningOracle, FLGatekeeper) deployed on \emph{both}
a gas-free (PureChain, PoA$^2$) and a gas-based (Polygon Amoy, PoS)
chain, the first side-by-side cost comparison across economic models;
and (4)~a dual-domain evaluation on a 9-client
N-BaIoT~\cite{meidan2018nbaiot} autoencoder federation, cross-validated
on a 5-client CASAS BiLSTM classifier, over three approximate-unlearning
paradigms plus exact retraining, measuring forgetting, on-chain cost,
and utility recovery.
