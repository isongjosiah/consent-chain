\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Federated learning (FL) trains a shared model across decentralised
clients without centralising their data~\cite{mcmahan2017fedavg} and is
often the only legally viable option in consumer IoT. Yet GDPR
Articles~7 and~17 demand more than privacy: consent must be
purpose-granular and revocable, with revocation propagating into the
model. Federated unlearning (FUL) erases a client's data and its
influence, but a bare withdrawal signal is a one-shot trigger, not an
enforced state: it cannot express purpose-scoped, time-bounded, or
partial consent, and nothing binds round selection to honour it, so
training may continue between request and claimed erasure, unrecorded.
Nor does running an algorithm evidence forgetting: approximate
unlearning, the only kind tractable at FL scale, gives no closed-form
guarantee, fails \emph{silently}, and is self-reported by the controller
that owes it. Membership-inference attacks
(MIAs)~\cite{shokri2017mia,carlini2022lira,choquettechoo2021labelonly}
detect this residual signal, and the check is not redundant: on CASAS,
gradient ascent, FedEraser, and even exact retraining leave a signal
above threshold (Section~\ref{sec:eval-casas}). \sysname therefore
couples \emph{dynamic consent}, an auditable state gating every round
\emph{before} training, with \emph{verifiable machine unlearning}, where
erasure must pass independent MIAs before consent advances. Prior
threads each supply one piece: consent ledgers
(MedRec~\cite{azaria2016medrec}) cannot gate training; unlearning
methods (SISA~\cite{bourtoule2021sisa}, FedEraser~\cite{liu2021federaser})
leave trigger and verification off-chain;
blockchain-FL~\cite{kim2020blockchained,li2020bflc} logs updates without
consent or forgetting verification.

\sysname makes four contributions: (1)~a Consent Lifecycle State Machine
(\clsm) of six states and nine transitions with four stated safety and
liveness properties, as a soulbound ERC-721; (2)~a Verifiable Unlearning
Protocol (\vup), method-agnostic over the unlearning algorithm,
accepting an attestation iff three \emph{independent} MIAs
(shadow~\cite{shokri2017mia}, LiRA~\cite{carlini2022lira},
label-only~\cite{choquettechoo2021labelonly}) fall below $\tauMIA$ under
$t$-of-$n$ ECDSA signatures; (3)~a three-contract Solidity architecture
(ConsentRegistry, UnlearningOracle, FLGatekeeper) deployed on \emph{both}
a gas-free (PureChain, PoA$^2$) and a gas-based (Polygon Amoy, PoS)
chain, the first side-by-side cost comparison across economic models;
and (4)~a dual-domain evaluation on a 9-client
N-BaIoT~\cite{meidan2018nbaiot} autoencoder federation, cross-validated
on a 5-client CASAS BiLSTM classifier, over three approximate-unlearning
paradigms plus exact retraining, measuring forgetting, on-chain cost,
and utility recovery.
