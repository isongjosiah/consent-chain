\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \sysname, the first protocol we are aware of to bind
dynamic consent, machine unlearning, and on-chain verification in a
single chain-enforced pipeline, closing the
consent--unlearning--verification triangle prior work addressed only at
the vertices: a formally-stated CLSM as soulbound ERC-721 tokens, a
method-agnostic VUP gated on three independent MIAs and a $t$-of-$n$
committee signature, and a three-contract architecture that runs
unchanged on gas-free and gas-based EVM chains. Evaluation on a
9-client N-BaIoT~\cite{meidan2018nbaiot} autoencoder, cross-validated
on 5-client CASAS, confirms four numbers: per-subject lifecycle cost
$\$0$ on PureChain versus $\$0.00120$ on Amoy; \texttt{validateRound}
scaling linearly to $N\!=\!500$ ($\$0$ versus a projected $\$0.0176$);
MIA scores driven below $\tauMIA\!=\!0.55$ by approximate unlearning;
and $\le\!3\%$ utility loss---indeed a $6.1\%$ \emph{gain} when the
revoked client was an outlier. Future work targets BLS aggregation,
zero-knowledge eligibility proofs, PoA$^2$-native verifiers, and
cross-chain consent portability.
