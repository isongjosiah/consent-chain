\section{The \sysname Framework}
\label{sec:framework}

\subsection{Verifiable Unlearning Protocol}
\label{sec:framework-vup}

Safety follows by inspection of $\delta$: no edge enters \textsc{Active}
from \textsc{Revoked}/\textsc{Expired}, only \texttt{unlearn\_verified}
is oracle-callable, and \texttt{validateRound}'s sole edge
($\mathtt{tick}$) guards on $\rho_r\in\mathcal{P}$. On transition to
\textsc{Revoked}/\textsc{Expired}, the gatekeeper sets
$\mathtt{pendingUnlearningSince}[i]=b$ and \texttt{validateRound} blocks
all rounds (liveness). Within $\dmax$ blocks the controller must
unlearn, score the post-unlearning model with the three MIAs, assemble
the Unlearning Attestation, gather $t$-of-$n$ signatures, and submit
it.

\subsubsection{Approximate unlearning methods}

\sysname is method-agnostic; we evaluate the three families introduced
in Section~\ref{sec:related}. SISA-Fed~\cite{bourtoule2021sisa}
retrains only the shards that touched $\mathit{DS}_i$'s data; gradient
ascent applies $k$ negative-learning-rate steps
$\theta^{(j+1)}=\theta^{(j)}+\eta\,\nabla\mathcal{L}(\theta^{(j)};
\mathcal{D}_i)$ ($k=10$, $\eta=10^{-4}$);
and FedEraser~\cite{liu2021federaser} replays the aggregation history
excluding $\mathit{DS}_i$ with a 3-round calibration.

\subsubsection{Three independent membership inference attacks}

We evaluate the post-unlearning model $f_{\mathrm{post}}$ against
$\mathit{DS}_i$'s training set $\mathcal{D}_i$ (member set $M$) with
three statistically independent attacks (so false negatives rarely
coincide), each scoring in $[0,1]$---higher meaning more residual
membership signal. The \emph{shadow-model}
attack~\cite{shokri2017mia}, adapted to reconstruction
models~\cite{hilprecht2019monte}, is the supervised baseline: $S=5$
shadow autoencoders yield ``in''/``out'' reconstruction errors that
train an MLP classifier, and $s_{\mathrm{sh}}$ is the fraction of $M$ it
flags ``in.'' \emph{LiRA}~\cite{carlini2022lira}, the strongest, calibrates a
\emph{per-example} likelihood ratio: for each $x\in M$ it fits
in-/out-pool Gaussians to the log-reconstruction-errors of
$n_{\mathrm{in}}=n_{\mathrm{out}}=4$ reference models, and
$s_{\mathrm{lira}}$ is the empirical TPR at an $\alpha$-controlled FPR
($\alpha=0.05$ canonical; $\alpha=0.5$ ablation in
Section~\ref{sec:discussion}). The \emph{label-only}
attack~\cite{choquettechoo2021labelonly}, seeing only the
reconstruction decision, thresholds at FPR $0.05$ and takes
$s_{\mathrm{lo}}$ as the fraction of $M$ below it.

\subsubsection{Unlearning Attestation and hash binding}

The Unlearning Attestation is the tuple
$u=\langle i, h_{\mathrm{pre}}, h_{\mathrm{post}}, m,
s_{\mathrm{sh}}^{\mathrm{fp}}, s_{\mathrm{lira}}^{\mathrm{fp}},
s_{\mathrm{lo}}^{\mathrm{fp}}, t_u\rangle$: the token ID, pre-/post-un\-learning
model hashes, method $m\in\{0,1,2\}$, fixed-point MIA scores
$s^{\mathrm{fp}}_j=\lfloor 10^4 s_j\rfloor$, and a timestamp. Every
verifier signs the UA hash
$h_u=\keccak(c \,\|\, i \,\|\, h_{\mathrm{pre}} \,\|\, h_{\mathrm{post}}
\,\|\, m \,\|\, s_{\mathrm{sh}}^{\mathrm{fp}} \,\|\,
s_{\mathrm{lira}}^{\mathrm{fp}} \,\|\, s_{\mathrm{lo}}^{\mathrm{fp}}
\,\|\, t_u)$, where $c=\texttt{block.chainid}$; including the chain
identifier $c$ binds the attestation to a single chain and prevents
cross-chain replay.

The oracle then accepts $(u,\Sigma)$ under four conditions: all three
fixed-point MIA scores are below $\tauMIA=0.55$ (i.e.\
$s^{\mathrm{fp}}_j<5500$); at least $t$ distinct registered verifiers,
recovered from the signatures $\Sigma$, signed $h_u$; the pre-hash
$h_{\mathrm{pre}}$ equals the model hash written once at revocation
($\mathit{CR}.\mathtt{modelHashAtRevocation}(i)$); and the token is
still \textsc{Revoked} or \textsc{Expired}---binding the UA to the
exact model version and blocking re-attestation of an
already-\textsc{Unlearned} token, closing the replay loop.

\subsection{Three-contract Solidity architecture}
\label{sec:framework-contracts}

\sysname is three Solidity 0.8.24 contracts (NatSpec in supplementary
\texttt{docs/contracts-spec.md}). \texttt{ConsentRegistry} is the
soulbound ERC-721 instantiating \clsm, adding a
write-once \texttt{modelHashAtRevocation} and a
\texttt{transitionToUnlearned} callable only by the oracle.
\texttt{UnlearningOracle} holds $\mathit{VC}$, $t$, and
$\tauMIA^{\mathrm{fp}}=5500$; \texttt{submitAttestation} checks the four
acceptance conditions and, on success, atomically finalises the token
and unblocks the gatekeeper. \texttt{FLGatekeeper} gates rounds:
\texttt{validateRound} reverts on any ineligible participant or pending
unlearning (encoding liveness), and permissionless \texttt{reportTimeout}
emits \texttt{ComplianceViolation} past the $\dmax$ deadline.

\paragraph{Automation loop} A revocation fires
\texttt{recordPendingUnlearning}; only a valid UA, via the oracle's
\texttt{unblock}, releases the gatekeeper---the closed
consent$\rightarrow$unlearn$\rightarrow$verify$\rightarrow$attest$\rightarrow$finalise
loop \sysname closes.
