\subsection{System model}

Six entities
$\langle \mathit{DS}_i, \mathit{DC}, \{\mathit{FL}_k\}, \mathit{VC},
\mathit{CR}, \mathit{UO}, \mathit{GK}\rangle$ compose \sysname: data
subject $\mathit{DS}_i$ (key-controlled owner of one soulbound Consent
NFT), controller $\mathit{DC}$ (drives the round loop, triggers
unlearning on revocation), devices $\mathit{FL}_k$ (local SGD), Verifier
Committee $\mathit{VC}=\{v_1,\dots,v_n\}$ ($n=5$ ECDSA keys, threshold
$t=3$), and the three contracts \texttt{ConsentRegistry} (CR),
\texttt{UnlearningOracle} (UO), \texttt{FLGatekeeper} (GK); any chain
reader is an \emph{auditor}. \sysname runs
FedAvg~\cite{mcmahan2017fedavg},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fedavg}
\theta^{(r+1)}=\sum_{k\in K_r}\frac{n_k}{\sum_{j\in K_r} n_j}\,
\theta^{(r+1)}_k,
\end{equation}
with the participant set $K_r$ in~\eqref{eq:fedavg} fixed to
$\mathit{GK}.\mathtt{validateRound}$'s return value, not an
orchestrator-chosen subset: consent is enforced at the membership, not
the gradient, level.

\subsection{Consent Lifecycle State Machine}
\label{sec:clsm-formal}

The \clsm is a deterministic finite automaton
$\clsm=(S,\Sigma,\delta,s_0,F)$ with start $s_0=\textsc{Ina}$, absorbing
terminal $F=\{\textsc{Unl}\}$, and states
$S=\{\textsc{Ina},\textsc{Act},\textsc{Sus},\textsc{Rev},\textsc{Exp},
\textsc{Unl}\}$ (inactive, active, suspended, revoked, expired,
unlearned). Its nine $\delta$-edges over the eight events $\Sigma$ are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:delta}
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{grant}\!:\textsc{Ina}\!\to\!\textsc{Act},\;\;
\{\texttt{modify},\texttt{tick}\}\!:\textsc{Act}\!\circlearrowleft,\\
&\texttt{revoke}\!:\textsc{Act}\!\to\!\textsc{Rev},\;\;
\texttt{expire}\!:\textsc{Act}\!\to\!\textsc{Exp},\\
&\texttt{suspend}/\texttt{resume}\!:\textsc{Act}\!\leftrightarrow\!\textsc{Sus},\\
&\texttt{unlearn\_verified}\!:\{\textsc{Rev},\textsc{Exp}\}\!\to\!\textsc{Unl}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Safety is immediate from~\eqref{eq:delta}: no edge targets \textsc{Act} from
$\{\textsc{Rev},\textsc{Exp}\}$, \texttt{unlearn\_verified} is
oracle-only, and the lone \texttt{validateRound} edge \texttt{tick}
guards on $\rho_r\in\mathcal{P}$. \texttt{ConsentRegistry}
realises $\delta$ one-edge-per-function; the soulbound constraint (no
\texttt{transferFrom}) confines token-$i$ transitions to $\mathit{DS}_i$.

\subsection{Verifiable Unlearning Protocol}
\label{sec:framework-vup}

On $\mathrm{state}(i)\!\to\!\{\textsc{Rev},\textsc{Exp}\}$ the gatekeeper
sets $\mathtt{pendingUnlearningSince}[i]=b$ and \texttt{validateRound}
blocks all rounds (liveness). Within $\dmax$ blocks the controller must
unlearn, score $f_{\mathrm{post}}$ with the three MIAs, assemble the
Unlearning Attestation, gather $t$-of-$n$ signatures, and submit.

\emph{Unlearning methods.} \sysname is method-agnostic over the three
families of Section~\ref{sec:related}:
SISA-Fed~\cite{bourtoule2021sisa} (per-shard retrain), gradient ascent
($\theta^{(j+1)}=\theta^{(j)}+\eta\,\nabla\mathcal{L}(\theta^{(j)};
\mathcal{D}_i)$, $k=10$ steps, $\eta=10^{-4}$), and
FedEraser~\cite{liu2021federaser} (history replay with 3-round
calibration).

\emph{Membership-inference attacks.} Against $\mathit{DS}_i$'s set $\mathcal{D}_i$ (members $M$), three
independent attacks each score $f_{\mathrm{post}}$ in $[0,1]$ (higher
$=$ more residual membership). The \emph{shadow-model}
attack~\cite{shokri2017mia}, adapted to reconstruction
models~\cite{hilprecht2019monte}, trains an MLP classifier on the
``in''/``out'' reconstruction errors of $S=5$ shadow autoencoders;
$s_{\mathrm{sh}}=$ the fraction of $M$ it flags ``in.'' \emph{LiRA}~\cite{carlini2022lira}, the strongest,
fits in-/out-pool Gaussians to the log-reconstruction-errors of
$n_{\mathrm{in}}=n_{\mathrm{out}}=4$ reference models per $x\in M$ and
takes $s_{\mathrm{lira}}=$ TPR at an $\alpha$-controlled FPR
($\alpha=0.05$; $\alpha=0.5$ ablation in Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).
The \emph{label-only} attack~\cite{choquettechoo2021labelonly}, seeing
only the reconstruction decision, thresholds at FPR $0.05$ and takes
$s_{\mathrm{lo}}=$ the fraction of $M$ below it.

\emph{Attestation and hash binding.} The Unlearning Attestation is the
tuple $u=\langle i, h_{\mathrm{pre}}, h_{\mathrm{post}}, m,
s_{\mathrm{sh}}^{\mathrm{fp}}, s_{\mathrm{lira}}^{\mathrm{fp}},
s_{\mathrm{lo}}^{\mathrm{fp}}, t_u\rangle$: token ID,
pre-/post-un\-learning model hashes, method $m\in\{0,1,2\}$, fixed-point
scores $s^{\mathrm{fp}}_j=\lfloor 10^4 s_j\rfloor$, timestamp. Each
verifier signs $h_u=\keccak(c \,\|\, u)$ over $c=\texttt{block.chainid}$,
binding the UA to one chain against cross-chain replay. Writing
$R(\Sigma)$ for the distinct registered signers of $h_u$ and
$h_{\mathrm{rev}}(i)=\mathit{CR}.\mathtt{modelHashAtRevocation}(i)$, the
oracle finalises iff
\begin{equation}\label{eq:accept}
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{accept}(u,\Sigma):\ &\Big(\textstyle\bigwedge_{j} s_j^{\mathrm{fp}}<5500\Big)
  \,\wedge\, |R(\Sigma)|\ge t\\[-1pt]
  &\wedge\; h_{\mathrm{pre}}=h_{\mathrm{rev}}(i)
  \,\wedge\, \mathrm{state}(i)\in\{\textsc{Rev},\textsc{Exp}\}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The last two conjuncts pin the UA to the exact model version and block
re-attestation of an already-\textsc{Unl} token, closing the replay
loop. With $\tauMIA^{\mathrm{fp}}=5500$ just above the $0.5$
random-guess baseline (margin for finite-$|M|$ noise), the three-attack
conjunction is what makes so tight a threshold tolerable.

\subsection{Three-contract Solidity architecture}
\label{sec:framework-contracts}

\sysname is three Solidity 0.8.24 contracts (NatSpec in supplementary
\texttt{docs/contracts-spec.md}). \texttt{ConsentRegistry}, the
soulbound ERC-721 realising $\delta$ above, adds a write-once
$h_{\mathrm{rev}}$ and a \texttt{transitionToUnlearned} callable only by
the oracle. \texttt{UnlearningOracle} holds $\mathit{VC}$, $t$, and
$\tauMIA^{\mathrm{fp}}$; \texttt{submitAttestation} evaluates
$\mathrm{accept}(u,\Sigma)$ and, on success, atomically finalises the
token and unblocks the gatekeeper. \texttt{FLGatekeeper} gates rounds:
\texttt{validateRound} reverts on any ineligible participant or pending
unlearning (encoding liveness), and permissionless \texttt{reportTimeout}
emits \texttt{ComplianceViolation} past the $\dmax$ deadline. The loop
closes when a revocation fires \texttt{recordPendingUnlearning} and only
a valid UA, via the oracle's \texttt{unblock}, releases the gatekeeper
(Figure~\ref{fig:arch}).

\subsection{Threat model}

We defend four in-scope adversaries: a \emph{lazy orchestrator} (GK
reverts on pending unlearning, emits \texttt{ComplianceViolation} after
$\dmax$), a \emph{deceptive controller} (three MIAs plus VC $t$-of-$n$
signatures), a \emph{consent forger} (soulbound NFT, \texttt{dsOwner}
check), and a \emph{replay attacker} (UA binding
$(c,i,h_{\mathrm{pre}})$). The committee is the trust floor: $\ge t$
colluding verifiers can certify an invalid attestation. Our journal
remedy stakes verifiers, slashable on a successful challenge:
$\mathit{DS}_i$, the one party holding $\mathcal{D}_i$ and motivated to
dispute, contests an accepted UA by exhibiting a contradicting MIA
evaluation against the attested
$(h_{\mathrm{pre}},h_{\mathrm{post}})$, with per-attestation committees
VRF-drawn from a larger pool against pre-collusion. Colluding participants (an
orthogonal DP layer) and six further classes are out of scope
(Section~\ref{sec:discussion}); the full per-adversary model is in
supplementary \texttt{docs/threat-model.md}.

\subsection{Formal properties}
\label{sec:properties}

\sysname satisfies four properties: the first pair by construction, the
second empirically (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}). \emph{Safety (consent
integrity):} \texttt{validateRound} admits only \textsc{Act} tokens with
$\rho_r\in\mathcal{P}_k$, and no $\delta$-path re-enters \textsc{Act}
(above). \emph{Audit completeness:} every transition, attestation,
violation, and model-hash update emits an indexed event.
\emph{Enforceability (liveness):} within $\dmax$ blocks of a revocation,
either a valid UA drives the token to \textsc{Unl} or the gatekeeper
logs a public \texttt{ComplianceViolation}, degrading to ``no
training,'' not ``unconsented training.'' \emph{Verifiability:} the
oracle finalises iff $\mathrm{accept}(u,\Sigma)$~\eqref{eq:accept} holds. Mechanised
proofs are left to future work.
