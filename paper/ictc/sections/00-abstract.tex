Federated learning over consumer IoT must now honour data-subject
withdrawal under GDPR, the EU AI Act, and CCPA: consent can be revoked
and the trained model must demonstrably forget. Prior work treats
consent management, machine unlearning, and on-chain auditability as
disjoint threads. \sysname closes this
\emph{consent--unlearning--verification triangle} in one chain-enforced
pipeline: a Consent Lifecycle State Machine as soulbound ERC-721 tokens,
a Verifiable Unlearning Protocol gating on-chain acceptance on three
independent membership-inference attacks, and a three-contract Solidity
architecture on gas-free PureChain (PoA$^2$) and gas-based Amoy (PoS).
Across N-BaIoT and CASAS, per-subject lifecycle cost is \$0 on PureChain
versus \$0.00120 on Amoy, and the gate accepts approximate unlearning
($\text{MIA}\!<\!0.55$) yet rejects naive retraining (whose
distributional uniqueness inflates MIA) and any attestation lacking a
3-of-5 verifier threshold, staying faithful to MIA evidence rather than
the controller's claim.
