\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \sysname, the first protocol we are aware of to bind
dynamic consent, machine unlearning, and on-chain verification into
a single chain-enforced pipeline --- closing the
consent--unlearning--verification triangle that prior work
addressed only at the vertices. The protocol decomposes into a
formally-stated Consent Lifecycle State Machine implemented as
soulbound ERC-721 tokens, a method-agnostic Verifiable Unlearning
Protocol whose acceptance predicate is the conjunction of three
independent MIA scores and a $t$-of-$n$ ECDSA committee signature,
and a three-contract Solidity architecture that runs unchanged on
gas-free and gas-based EVM substrates. End-to-end empirical
evaluation on a 9-client N-BaIoT~\cite{meidan2018nbaiot} federated
autoencoder confirms the four headline numbers: per-data-subject
on-chain lifecycle cost of $\$0$ on PureChain versus $\$0.00120$ on
Polygon Amoy, $\texttt{validateRound}$ scaling linearly to
$N\!=\!500$ at $\$0$ on PureChain versus a projected $\$0.0176$ on
Amoy, MIA scores driven below the on-chain $\tauMIA\!=\!0.55$
threshold by all three approximate unlearning methods, and an
absolute utility delta of $\le\!3\%$ on two of three methods --- in
fact a $5.7\%$ utility \emph{gain} when the revoked client was an
outlier in the benign distribution. Future work targets BLS
threshold-signature aggregation, zero-knowledge consent eligibility
proofs, PoA$^2$-native verifier-committee integration, and
cross-chain consent portability for federations spanning multiple
EVM chains.
